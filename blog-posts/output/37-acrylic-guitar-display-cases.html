Why Acrylic for Guitar Display?

Glass display cases are heavy, fragile, and — for anything larger than a jewelry box — a logistical headache. Acrylic changes the equation entirely.

92% light transmission — clearer than glass, with none of the greenish tint visible in thick glass panels. Your guitar's finish, grain, and hardware are displayed with absolute color fidelity.

10–20x more impact resistant than glass. An acrylic case won't shatter if bumped, knocked, or jostled during a move. For homes with kids, pets, or foot traffic near the display, this isn't optional — it's essential.

Half the weight of glass. Wall-mounted displays become practical. Freestanding floor cases don't require reinforced shelving. Shipping and installation are dramatically simpler.

UV protection. UV-stabilized acrylic blocks the ultraviolet light that fades finishes, yellows lacquer, and degrades vintage instruments over time. Your guitar looks the same in five years as it does today.

A well-made acrylic display case isn't just a box around a guitar. It's museum-grade protection with showroom-grade clarity — and it's built to the exact dimensions of your instrument.

03

The Fabrication Process

Material selection: Cases are fabricated from cast acrylic — chosen for its superior optical clarity, tighter thickness tolerances, and better resistance to crazing over time compared to extruded grades. UV-stabilized grades are specified for cases that will be exposed to sunlight or strong indoor lighting.

Precision cutting: Panels are CNC-cut to exact dimensions. For cases with curved or contoured elements, thermoforming produces smooth, seamless bends without joints.

Solvent welding: Panels are chemically bonded using Weld-On solvent cements, producing joints that are optically clear and structurally as strong as the panel material. No visible seams, no cloudy glue lines, no silicone.

Polishing: All edges and joints are polished to optical clarity. The finished case should be visually invisible — the guitar is the star, not the enclosure.

How to Order

The easiest path to a quote is to provide the overall dimensions of the guitar (length, width at widest point, and depth including hardware), the display style you prefer (full enclosure, wall-mount, floor-standing), and any special requirements (UV protection, ventilation, engraved nameplate, LED-ready edge-lit panel).

If you're not sure about dimensions, send us a photo of the guitar with a tape measure or ruler in frame — we'll take it from there.
