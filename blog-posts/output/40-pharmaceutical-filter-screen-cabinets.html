The Problem: Expensive Screens, No Proper Storage

In pharmaceutical manufacturing, sifting and filter screens are precision instruments — gauged to exact mesh specifications, calibrated for specific particle sizes, and essential to product quality and batch consistency. They're also surprisingly fragile. A bent frame, a torn mesh, or contamination from improper storage can take a screen out of service and halt a production run.

And yet, in many plants, these critical screens are stored leaning against walls, stacked on shelves, or tucked into corners where they're exposed to casual damage from foot traffic, forklift contact, cleaning operations, and unauthorized handling.

The cost of a custom storage cabinet is a fraction of the cost of replacing damaged screens — and a rounding error compared to the cost of a production delay caused by a screen that isn't available when it's needed.

The Solution: Custom Polypropylene Cabinets on Casters

Plastic-Craft Products fabricated this pharmaceutical filter screen cabinet from white stress-relieved polypropylene sheet in cooperation with plant engineers. The material was specified for its chemical resistance, cleanability, and compliance with pharmaceutical manufacturing environment requirements.

Custom-Sized Dividers

Internal dividers sized and spaced for each gauged screen in the plant's inventory. Every screen has a dedicated slot — no stacking, no contact between screens, no searching.

Casters for Mobility

Transportable from storage to production floor to cleaning station and back. Screens travel with their cabinet, never loose on a cart or carried by hand.

Locking Doors

Security locks keep unauthorized hands off critical equipment. Doors close to protect screens from dust, splash, and incidental contact during storage.

Chemical-Resistant Material

White stress-relieved polypropylene resists the cleaning agents and chemicals present in pharmaceutical manufacturing environments. Wipes clean without degradation.

03

Key Features

Ready to use when needed: Screens are stored upright in individual slots, organized by gauge. When production calls for a specific screen, it's immediately identifiable and accessible — no sorting through a stack.

Custom-sized for items to be stored: The cabinet dimensions, divider spacing, and slot widths are engineered around the specific screens in your inventory. Not a generic storage unit repurposed — a purpose-built solution.

Transportable from point to point: Heavy-duty casters allow the cabinet to be rolled from storage to the production line, to the cleaning area, and back to storage. Screens never leave the cabinet during transport.

Security locks and closures: Locking doors prevent unauthorized access. In regulated manufacturing environments, controlling who handles process-critical equipment isn't just good practice — it's a compliance requirement.
