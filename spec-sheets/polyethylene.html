The polyethylene family offers the best chemical resistance of any commodity thermoplastic at the lowest cost. LDPE provides maximum flexibility and impact toughness; HDPE adds stiffness, tensile strength, and higher temperature resistance. Both are FDA/USDA compliant and non-toxic.

LDPE Gaskets, flexible liners, squeeze bottles, soft bumpers, packaging

HDPE Tanks, cutting boards, wear parts, marine, structural components
