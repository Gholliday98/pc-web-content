FDA & Food Safety Compliance: Our HDPE cutting board stock is manufactured exclusively from virgin FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 compliant resin — no recycled content, no fillers, no additives that could leach into food. The non-porous, closed-cell surface prevents bacterial harborage and is approved for direct food contact by the FDA, USDA, and NSF. Boards withstand commercial dishwashers (up to 180°F) and all standard sanitizing agents including bleach, quaternary ammonium, and peracetic acid. Can be resurfaced (sanded) multiple times to restore a like-new cutting surface, extending service life and reducing replacement costs.
Fabrication Notes: Excellent machinability — cut, rout, drill with standard tooling  |  Weldable by hot plate & hot gas  |  Not bondable with adhesives — use mechanical fasteners  |  Thermoformable  |  Lightweight (floats in water)
Chemical Resistance: Excellent resistance to acids, bases, alcohols, sanitizers, and food-grade cleaning agents. Avoid: strong oxidizing acids and aromatic/chlorinated solvents at elevated temperatures.
