Premium bearing-grade nylon with molybdenum disulfide (MoS₂) for enhanced lubricity and wear resistance. Hydlar Z delivers significantly lower friction and improved dimensional stability over standard nylon — the first choice for high-load bearings, gears, and wear components operating without external lubrication.

Rods, sheets, and tubes in standard sizes. Recognizable by its dark gray/black color. Most common sizes ship from stock.
